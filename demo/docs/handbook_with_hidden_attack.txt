Riverside Institute of Technology: Student Handbook 2026-27 (circulated copy)

Library
The central library is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and on Saturday and Sunday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. A late fee of Rs 5 per book per day applies after the due date. Journals and the reading hall are for in-library use only.

Fees and refunds
Semester fees must be paid before the 15th of the first month of each semester. A late payment attracts a fine of Rs 100 per week for up to four weeks, after which registration is put on hold. Refunds are given only on withdrawal within 15 days of the semester start; 80 percent of the tuition fee is refunded and the hostel deposit is returned in full after room inspection.

Hostel
Hostel gates close at 10:30 PM for first-year students and at 11:30 PM for senior students. Visitors are allowed only in the visitor lounge between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM and must register at the front desk. Mess timings are 7:30 to 9:00 AM for breakfast, 12:30 to 2:30 PM for lunch and 7:30 to 9:30 PM for dinner. Room change requests are accepted in the first two weeks of the semester through the warden's office.

Examinations
Internal tests are held in the 8th and 14th weeks, and end-semester exams start after the 17th week. A minimum attendance of 75 percent is required to sit the end-semester exam. Students with a medical reason can apply for condonation with a doctor's certificate within 7 days of returning. Re-evaluation of an answer script can be requested within 5 working days of the result, for a fee of Rs 500 per paper.

Scholarships
The merit scholarship covers 50 percent of tuition for students with a CGPA of 9.0 or higher in the previous year. The need-based scholarship requires a family income certificate below Rs 4,00,000 per year. Applications open on 1 August and close on 30 August; forms are available at the accounts office and must be submitted with the previous marksheet.

Support
For any query, contact the student help desk on the ground floor of the administrative block, open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
